01 / BUDGET

Know your daily budget

Remaining quota ÷ days until reset. With less than a day left, the overlay shows “Until reset” and the remaining allowance.

02 / PACE

Keep your pace in check

Compare your balance with an even 7-day pace. This demo allows a margin of 1% of your total weekly quota: falling further behind turns the status amber; an empty balance turns it red.

03 / SCOPE

Track the weekly allowance

Short-term limits may still apply. This design provides budget and pace guidance. When the reset is due, it asks for a refresh rather than assuming the balance has recovered.
